# Code of conduct

Open Worlds is run by volunteers, for people who host games for their friends.
Keep it a place people want to come back to.

## Expected

- Be kind and assume good faith, especially with people new to the project.
- Criticise code and ideas, not the people who wrote them.
- Accept a "no" on a proposal gracefully, and give reasons when you say one.

## Not accepted

- Harassment, insults, or personal or political attacks.
- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome attention of any kind.
- Discrimination based on who someone is.
- Publishing someone else's private information without their permission.
- Continuing after someone has asked you to stop.

## Scope

This applies in the repository (issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews)
and wherever someone represents the project.

## Reporting

Report a problem privately to the maintainer, [@kyler-frazier](https://github.com/kyler-frazier),
using the contact on their GitHub profile. Reports are kept confidential.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close issues and pull requests, and
block people from the project, temporarily or for good, for behaviour that
breaks this code. Usually a warning comes first; serious or repeated cases skip
it.

Adapted in spirit from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/).
